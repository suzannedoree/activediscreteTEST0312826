Item (b).

Expand \((r-1)S\) and simplify your answer.
Solution.
We get
\begin{align*} (r-1)S \amp = (r-1)(a + ar + ar^2 + ar^3 + ar^4 + ar^5 + ar^ 6)\\ \amp = (ar + ar^2 + ar^3 + ar^4 + ar^5 + ar^6 + ar^ 7) - (a + ar + ar^2 + ar^3 + ar^4 + ar^5 + ar^ 6)\\ \amp = \cancel{ar} + \cancel{ar^2} + \cancel{ar^3} + \cancel{ar^4} + \cancel{ar^5} + \cancel{ar^6} + ar^7 - a - \cancel{ar} - \cancel{ar^2} - \cancel{ar^3} - \cancel{ar^4} - \cancel{ar^5} - \cancel{ar^ 6})\\ \amp = ar^7-a\\ \amp = a(r^7-1) \end{align*}
🔗
Thus, \((r-1)S = a(r^7-1)\text{.}\)
