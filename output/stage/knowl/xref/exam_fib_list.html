Example 8.5.2. The Fibonacci sequence.

Find the first twenty terms of the Fibonacci sequence: \(F_0, F_1, \ldots, F_{19}\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
The initial conditions tell us that \(F_0=0\) and \(F_1=1\text{.}\) Next, we use the recursion with \(n=2\) to get
\begin{equation*} F_2=F_1+F_0 = 1+0=1\text{.} \end{equation*}
🔗
Continuing to use the recursion with \(n=\) 3, 4, 5 and 6 we get
\begin{equation*} F_3=F_2+F_1 = 1+1=2\text{,} \end{equation*}
\begin{equation*} F_4=F_3+F_2 = 2+1=3\text{,} \end{equation*}
\begin{equation*} F_5=F_4+F_3 = 3+2=5\text{,} \end{equation*}
and
\begin{equation*} F_6=F_5+F_4 = 5+3=8\text{.} \end{equation*}
🔗
We continue this pattern to get the rest of the first twenty terms as listed in Table 8.5.3. For example, when \(n=15\) we have
\begin{equation*} F_{15}=F_{14}+F_{13} = 377+233=610\text{.} \end{equation*}
