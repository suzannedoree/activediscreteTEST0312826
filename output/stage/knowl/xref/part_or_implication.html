Item (a).

Copy the following proof of proof of an “or” statement using the implication equivalence. Note that we are using Theorem 5.2.16 because the statement is universally quantified as well as Theorem 5.3.16. Prove that for any positive real numbers \(a\) and \(b\) such that \(ab=100\text{,}\) either \(a \le 10\) or \(b \lt 10\text{.}\)

Proof.
Let \(a\) and \(b\) be positive real numbers such that \(ab = 100\text{.}\) Assume \(a > 10\text{.}\) Multiplying each side of this equation by the positive number \(b\) we get \(100=ab > 10b\text{.}\) Dividing each side of this equation by 10 we get \(b \lt 10\text{.}\) Thus either \(a \le 10\) or \(b \lt 10\text{.}\)
