Item (c).

Copy the following proof using mathematical induction to prove that \(\displaystyle \prod_{k=1}^nk=n!\) for \(n \ge 1\text{.}\)

Proof.
Write \(f(n) = n!\text{.}\) Induct on \(n\text{.}\)
🔗
First, when \(n=1\) we know that \(\displaystyle \prod_{k=1}^1k=1=1\) and also \(f(1)=1!=1\text{.}\)
🔗
Next, assume \(\displaystyle \prod_{k=1}^{n-1}k=f(n-1)= (n-1)!\) for some integer \(n > s\text{.}\)
🔗
Then
\begin{align*} \prod_{k=1}^nk \amp = \left(\prod_{k=1}^{n-1}k\right) \cdot n \\ \amp = f(n-1) \cdot n \\ \amp = \left((n-1)!\right) \cdot n \\ \amp = n(n-1)! =n! = f(n) \end{align*}
