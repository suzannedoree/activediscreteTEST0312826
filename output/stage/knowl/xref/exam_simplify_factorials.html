Example 2.2.18. Simplifying \(n(n-1)!\)

 
  1. Write as a single factorial: \(7(6!)\)
    Solution.
    We have
    \begin{equation*} 7(6!) = (7)\underbrace{(6)(5)(4)(3)(2)(1)}_{6!} = \underbrace{(7)(6)(5)(4)(3)(2)(1)}_{7!}=7! \end{equation*}
