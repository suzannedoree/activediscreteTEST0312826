Example 3.2.27. Adjacency matrix.

Find an adjacency matrix of the graph \(S\) from Activity 3.2.A.
🔗
Solution.
\(S\) has an adjacency matrix shown in Table 3.2.28.
🔗
Notice that the matrix depends on the order of the vertices, here shown in alphabetical order. To obtain the first row of this matrix, we note that the vertex \(v_1\) is adjacent to the vertices \(v_2\) and \(v_4\text{.}\) Thus, \(A_{1,2}=\bs{1}\) and \(A_{1,4}=\bs{1}\) and the other entries in the first row are \(\bs{0}\text{.}\) Similarly, to get the second row, we note that \(v_2\) is adjacent to \(v_1\text{,}\) \(v_3\text{,}\) \(v_4\text{,}\) and \(v_5\text{.}\) Thus, \(A_{2,1}=\bs{1}\text{,}\) \(A_{2,3}=\bs{1}\text{,}\) \(A_{2,4}=\bs{1}\text{,}\) and \(A_{2,5}=\bs{1}\) and the other entries are \(\bs{0}\text{.}\) The other rows follow in a similar way.
