Activity 8.2.C. Use mathematical induction to prove the explicit form of a recursively-defined sequence.

 
  1. Copy the following proof. Consider the sequence \(a\) defined recursively by \(a_0= 1\) and \(a_n = 2a_{n-1}\) for \(n \ge 1\text{.}\) Prove that \(a_n=2^n\) for \(n \ge 0\text{.}\)

    Proof.
    Write \(f(n) = 2^n\text{.}\) Induct on \(n\text{.}\)
    🔗
    First, when \(n=0\) we know that \(a_0 = 1\) and also \(f(0) = 2^0 = 1\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Next, assume \(a_{n-1} = f(n-1)= 2^{n-1}\) for some integer \(n \ge 0\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Then \(a_n = 2a_{n-1} = 2 \cdot 2^{n-1} = 2^n = f(n)\) \(\Box\)
    🔗
