13. Understand.

For the matrix \(J=\left[ \begin{array}{cc} 1 \amp 1 \\ 1 \amp 1 \end{array} \right]\text{,}\) use mathematical induction to prove that \(J^n=\left[ \begin{array}{cc} 2^{n-1} \amp 2^{n-1} \\ 2^{n-1} \amp 2^{n-1} \end{array} \right]\) for \(n\ge 1\text{.}\)
🔗
Check.

Proof.
Write \(M_n=\left[ \begin{array}{cc} 2^{n-1} \amp 2^{n-1} \\ 2^{n-1} \amp 2^{n-1} \end{array} \right]\text{.}\) Induct on \(n\text{.}\)
🔗
First, when \(n=1\) we have \(J^1 = J = \left[ \begin{array}{cc} 1 \amp 1 \\ 1 \amp 1 \end{array} \right]\) and also \(M_1=\left[ \begin{array}{cc} 2^0 \amp 2^0\\ 2^0 \amp 2^0 \end{array} \right]= \left[ \begin{array}{cc} 1 \amp 1 \\ 1 \amp 1 \end{array} \right]\text{.}\)
🔗
Next, assume \(J^{n-1} = M_{n-1} = \left[ \begin{array}{cc} 2^{n-2} \amp 2^{n-2} \\ 2^{n-2} \amp 2^{n-2} \end{array} \right]\) for some integer \(n \ge 2\text{.}\) Then
\begin{equation*} J^n = JJ^{n-1} =AM_{n-1} = \left[ \begin{array}{cc} 1 \amp 1 \\ 1 \amp 1 \end{array} \right]\left[ \begin{array}{cc} 2^{n-2} \amp 2^{n-2}\\ 2^{n-2} \amp 2^{n-2} \end{array} \right] = \ldots =M_n \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗
Fill in the missing algebra using \(2^{n-2}+2^{n-2} = 2 \cdot 2^{n-2} = 2^{n-1}\text{.}\)
