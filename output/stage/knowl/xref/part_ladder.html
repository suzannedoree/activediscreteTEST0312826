Item (g).

The ladder \(L_{2\times5}\) consists of two copies of the path \(P_5\) with \(5\) additional edges between each pair of corresponding vertices. Officially, if the vertex set is \(\{v_1,v_2,v_3,v_4,v_5,,w_1,w_2,w_3,w_4,w_5\}\text{,}\) then the edges are
top rail: \(\{v_1,v_2\}, \{v_2,v_3\}, \{v_3,v_4\}, \{v_4,v_5\}\)
bottom rail: \(\{w_1,w_2\}, \{w_2,w_3\}, \{w_3,w_4\},\{w_4,w_5\}\)
rungs: \(\{v_1,w_1\}, \{v_2,w_2\}, \{v_3,w_3\},\{v_4,w_4\}, \{v_5,w_5\}\text{.}\)
