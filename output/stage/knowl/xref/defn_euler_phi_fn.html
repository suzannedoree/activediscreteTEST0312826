Definition 4.5.8. Euler’s totient.

The number of integers from 1 to \(n\) that are coprime to \(n\) is denoted \(\varphi(n)\text{.}\) In this context, \(\varphi\) is Euler’s toitent (or Euler’s \(\varphi\) function).
 3 
\(\varphi\) is the Greek letter phi, sometimes written as \(\phi\text{.}\)
