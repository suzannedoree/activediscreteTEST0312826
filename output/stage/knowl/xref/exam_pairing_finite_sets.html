Example 6.3.11. Pairing finite sets.

Give an example of a pairing of the sets \(X=\{1,2,3,4\}\) and \(Y=\{a,b,c,d\}\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
A possible pairing is
\begin{equation*} 1 \leftrightarrow a, 2 \leftrightarrow b, 3 \leftrightarrow c, 4 \leftrightarrow d\text{.} \end{equation*}
Another pairing is
\begin{equation*} 1 \leftrightarrow b, 2 \leftrightarrow c, 3 \leftrightarrow d, 4 \leftrightarrow a\text{.} \end{equation*}
We verify this example satisfies the definition of a pairing in Definition 6.3.12.
