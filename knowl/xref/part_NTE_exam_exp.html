Item (e).

The sequence \(e\) begins \(2,20,200,2000,20000, \ldots\text{.}\)
Solution.
We multiply by 10 to get from each term to the next, so the answer likely involves \(10 \times 10 \times \ldots \times 10 = 10^n\text{.}\) Let’s make a table comparing \(n\text{,}\) \(10^n\text{,}\) and the terms of the sequence starting at \(n=0\text{,}\) in Table 6.5.11. We can get from \(10^n\) to the terms by multiplying by two. A possible formula is \(e_n = 2(10^n)\) for \(n \ge 0\text{.}\)
