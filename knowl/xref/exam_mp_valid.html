Example 5.5.16. Why modus ponens is valid.

We proved modus ponens in Activity 5.5.A (c) using equivalences. In this example, we look at two different ways to show that modus ponens is a valid inference.
  1. Explain in words why modus ponens makes sense.
    Solution.
    Suppose we know that \(P\) is true and that \(P \to Q\) is true. The statement \(P \to Q\) promises that if \(P\) is true, then \(Q\) is guaranteed to be true. Since \(P\) is indeed true, it follows that \(Q\) is true.
