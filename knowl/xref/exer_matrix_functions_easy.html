  1. Consider the function \(f: \mathbb{Z} \to \mathscr{M}(\mathbb{Z})\) defined by \(f(n) = \left[ \begin{array}{cc} n \amp 0 \\ 0 \amp 2n \end{array} \right]\text{.}\) Identify the domain and codomain of \(f\) and evaluate \(f(3)\text{.}\)
