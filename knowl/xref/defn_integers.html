Definition 2.1.6. Integers.

 
  1. The numbers \(\ldots, -3, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 3, \ldots\) are integers.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. The numbers \(0, 1, 2, 3, \ldots\) are natural numbers (or non-negative integers)
     1 
    You may find that other mathematics classes have the natural numbers starting with \(1\)
    . For example, the phrase “for any natural number \(n\)” means “for any integer \(n \ge 0\)”.
    🔗
    🔗
  3. The numbers \(1, 2, 3, \ldots\) are positive integers. For example, the phrase “for any positive integer \(n\)” means “for any integer \(n \ge 1\)”.
    🔗
    🔗
