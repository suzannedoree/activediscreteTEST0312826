Theorem 5.4.7. Proof Format: Biconditional.

We can prove \(P \leftrightarrow Q\) by adapting the following proof format.
🔗

Proof.
First, assume \(P\text{.}\)
🔗
(Explain why \(Q\) is true.)
🔗
Thus, \(Q\text{.}\)
🔗
Conversely, assume \(Q\text{.}\)
🔗
(Explain why \(P\) is true.)
🔗
Thus, \(P\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
