Theorem 9.5.7. Linearity properties of sums.

Let \(a_k\) and \(b_k\) be sequences of real numbers for \(k \ge s\) for some nonnegative integer \(s\) and let \(c\) be a real number. Then
  1. \(\displaystyle \sum_{k=s}^n(ca_k)=c\sum_{k=s}^na_k\text{.}\)

  2. \(\displaystyle \sum_{k=s}^n(a_k+b_k)=\sum_{k=s}^na_k+\sum_{k=s}^nb_k\text{.}\)
