Theorem 6.4.20. Proof Format: Uniqueness (Direct).

We can prove there is at most one \(n\) with \(P(n)\) directly by adapting the following proof format.
🔗

Proof.
Assume \(P(n)\text{.}\)
🔗
(Explain why \(n = k\text{.}\))
🔗
Thus, if \(P(n)\text{,}\) then \(n = k\text{.}\) It follows that \(k\) is the only possible value of \(n\text{.}\)
🔗
Therefore, there is at most one \(n\) with \(P(n)\text{,}\) namely \(k\text{.}\)
