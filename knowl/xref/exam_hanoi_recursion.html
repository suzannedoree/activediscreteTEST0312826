Example 8.1.2. Tower of Hanoi puzzle.

Consider the sequence \(h\) where \(h_n\) is the minimum number of moves needed to solve the puzzle with \(n\) disks for \(n \ge 1\text{.}\) The minimum number of moves needed to solve the puzzle with \(n-1\) disks would then be \(h_{n-1}\text{.}\) Make a conjecture about a rule for \(h_n\) in terms of \(h_{n-1}\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
To solve the puzzle for \(n\) disks, we can break the process into three phases.
  • Phase 1: Relocate the top \(n-1\) disks in the pile to Tower 2. [\(h_{n-1}\) moves]
    🔗
    🔗
  • Phase 2: Move the largest disk to Tower 3. [1 move]
    🔗
    🔗
  • Phase 3: Relocate the top \(n-1\) disks in the pile to Tower 3. [\(h_{n-1}\) moves]
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
This analysis tells us that
\begin{equation*} h_n=2h_{n-1}+1\text{.} \end{equation*}
