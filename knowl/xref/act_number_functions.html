Activity 6.2.B. Evaluating number-theoretic functions.

 
  1. The function
    \begin{equation*} \fn{twopart}: \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N} \end{equation*}
    defined by \(\fn{twopart}(n)\) is the highest power of two that divides \(n\text{.}\) Note that \(1 = 2^0\) is a power of two. Evaluate \(\fn{twopart}(24)\text{,}\) \(\fn{twopart}(30)\text{,}\) and \(\fn{twopart}(31)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. The function
    \begin{equation*} \pi: \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N} \end{equation*}
    defined by \(\pi(n)=\) the number of primes less than or equal to \(n\text{.}\) Note that the function \(\pi\) has nothing to do with the real number \(\pi\text{.}\) Evaluate \(\pi(6)\) and \(\pi(20)\text{.}\)
