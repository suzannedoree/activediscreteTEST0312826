Give a combinatorial proof that
\begin{equation*} \sum_{k=1}^nk = \binom{n+1}{2} \end{equation*}
for \(n \ge 1\text{.}\) Hint: How many 2-elements subsets does \(S = \{0, 1, 2, \ldots, n\}\) have? Condition on \(k =\) the larger number in the subset. Note that the other number in the subset must come from \(\{0, 1, 2, \ldots, k-1\}\text{.}\)
