Example 9.2.2. Ternary Conversion.

 
  1. Convert \((21021)_3\) to decimal.
    Solution.
    To determine the highest power of three involved, we count in from the right: \(3^0, 3^1, 3^2, 3^3\text{,}\) and finally \(3^4\text{.}\) We have
    \begin{align*} (21021)_3 \amp =\underline{2}\cdot 3^4 +\underline{1}\cdot 3^3 +\underline{0}\cdot 3^2 +\underline{2}\cdot 3^1 +\underline{1}\cdot 3^0\\ \amp =\underline{2}\cdot 81 +\underline{1}\cdot 27 +\underline{0}\cdot 9 +\underline{2}\cdot 3 +\underline{1}\cdot 1\\ \amp = 196\text{.} \end{align*}

  2. Convert 50 to base three.
    Solution.
    Since \(50 \lt 81 = 3^4\text{,}\) we only need to use 27, nine, three, and one. Note that \(50-27 = 23\text{,}\) \(23-9-9 = 5\text{,}\) \(5-3=2\text{,}\) and \(2=1+1\) and so we get
    \begin{align*} 50 \amp = 27 + 9 + 9 + 3 + 1 + 1\\ \amp =\underline{1}\cdot 27 +\underline{2}\cdot 9 + \underline{1}\cdot 3 + \underline{2}\cdot 1\\ \amp = \underline{1}\cdot 3^3 + \underline{2}\cdot 3^2 + \underline{1} \cdot 3^1 + \underline{2} \cdot 3^0\\ \amp = (1212)_3 \end{align*}
