Item 21.(a).

We deposit \(10,000\) into a bank account that earns 3% interest annually. Write \(a_n=\) the account balance at the end of \(n\) years. Note that interest is paid at the end of each year. Calculate \(a_0\text{,}\) \(a_1\text{,}\) and \(a_2\) and write a recursive rule for \(a_n\text{.}\) Simplify your answer using \(1 + .03 = 1.03\text{.}\) Note: you might know an explicit rule, but this question asks for a recursive rule, so \(a_{n-1}\) should be involved in your answer.
