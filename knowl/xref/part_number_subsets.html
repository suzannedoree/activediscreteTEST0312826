Item (c).

How many subsets does \(A = \{1,2,3,4,5\}\) have?
Solution.
The phrase “how many” indicates that we are counting. Let’s think about how to select a subset \(S\) of \(A\text{.}\)
🔗
Step 1: decide if \(1 \in S\text{.}\) There are 2 choices: yes or no. (2 ways)
🔗
Step 2: decide if \(2 \in S\text{.}\) There are again 2 choices: yes or no. (2 ways)
🔗
Step 3: decide if \(3 \in S\text{.}\) (2 ways).
🔗
Step 4: decide if \(4 \in S\text{.}\) (2 ways).
🔗
Step 5: decide if \(5 \in S\text{.}\) (2 ways).
🔗
Since steps multiply Theorem 2.2.2, there are \(2\cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 = 2^5\) subsets.
