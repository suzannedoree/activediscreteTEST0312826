Theorem 5.2.12. Proof Format: Existentially-quantified statements.

We can prove \(\exists n ~ P(n)\) by adapting the following proof format.
🔗

Proof.
Consider \(n=\) (example).
🔗
(Explain why \(P(n)\) is true.)
🔗
