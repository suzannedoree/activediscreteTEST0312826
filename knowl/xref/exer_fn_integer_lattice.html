11. Understand.

Consider the function \(s: \mathbb{Z}^2 \to \mathbb{Z}^2\) defined by \(s(a,b) = (a+b,a-b)\text{.}\)
  1. Evaluate \(s(1,2), s(-2,5)\text{,}\) and \(s(0,0)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Find integers \(a,b \in \mathbb{Z}\) such that \(s(a,b) = (8,2)\text{.}\) Repeat for \(s(a,b) = (-3,5)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Show that there do not exist integers \(a,b\) such that \(s(a,b) = (2,1)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
Check.
  1. Hint: \(s(1,2)=(3,-1)\text{.}\)

  2. Hint: For \(s(a,b) = (8,2)\) we want integers \(a\) and \(b\) such that \(a+b=8\) and \(a-b=2\text{.}\) The second equation tells us \(a = b+2\text{.}\) Either guess from here or plug into the first equation to get \((b+2)+b=8\text{.}\) Solve for \(b\text{.}\)
    🔗
