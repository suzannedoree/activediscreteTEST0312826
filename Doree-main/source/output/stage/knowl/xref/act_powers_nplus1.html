Activity 9.4.3. Powers of \(n+1\).

 
  1. Expand and simplify \((n+1)^2\text{.}\) Hint: Start by writing \((n+1)^2=(n+1)(n+1)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Expand and simplify \((n+1)^3\text{.}\) Hint: Multiply \((n+1)\) times your answer to Item 1.
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Check that you found the first two formulas listed here:
    \begin{align*} (n+1)^2 \amp =n^2+2n+1\\ (n+1)^3 \amp =n^3 +3n^2+3n+1\\ (n+1)^4 \amp =n^4+4n^3+6n^2+4n+1 \end{align*}
    We provided the third equation so that you have another example. Based on these examples, what is the connection between \((n+1)^m\) and the binomial coefficients? Explain in words.
    🔗
    🔗
  4. Conjecture a formula for \((n+1)^m\) using summation notation. Hint: The powers on \(n\) decrease, so try using \(x^{m-k}\) in your formula.
