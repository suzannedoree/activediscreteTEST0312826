Definition 3.2.20. Wheels and ladders.

 
  1. For \(s \ge 3\text{,}\) the wheel, denoted \(W_s\text{,}\) consists of the cycle \(C_s\) with an additional hub vertex that is adjacent to each vertex in the cycle. For example, Figure 3.2.21 shows a drawing of \(W_3\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. For \(t \ge 1\text{,}\) ladder, denoted \(L_{2\times t}\text{,}\) consists of two copies of the path \(P_t\) with \(t\) additional edges between each pair of corresponding vertices. Officially, if the vertex set is \(\{v_1,v_2,\ldots,v_t,w_1,w_2,\ldots,w_t\}\text{,}\) then the edges are
    top rail: \(\{v_1,v_2\}, \{v_2,v_3\}, \ldots, \{v_{t-1},v_t\}\)
    bottom rail: \(\{w_1,w_2\}, \{w_2,w_3\}, \ldots, \{w_{t-1},w_t\}\)
    rungs: \(\{v_1,w_1\}, \{v_2,w_2\}, \ldots, \{v_t,w_t\}\text{.}\)
    For example, Figure 3.2.22 shows a drawing of \(L_{2 \times 4}\text{.}\)
