Example 3.2.23. Wheels and ladders.

 
  1. Draw the wheels \(W_4\) and \(W_5\text{.}\) For each graph count the number of vertices and edges, and list the degree sequence.
    Solution.
    Figure 3.2.24 shows a drawing of the graphs. The wheel \(W_4\) has five vertices and eight edges. The hub vertex has degree four and each of the outer vertices has degree three. Its degree sequence is \(4,3,3,3,3\text{.}\)
    🔗
    The wheel \(W_5\) has six vertices and ten edges. The hub vertex has degree five and each of the outer vertices has degree three. Its degree sequence is \(5,3,3,3,3,3\text{.}\)

  2. Draw the ladder \(L_{2\times5}\text{,}\) count the number of vertices and edges, and list the degree sequence.
    Solution.
    Figure 3.2.25 shows a drawing of \(L_{2\times 5}\) which has ten vertices and thirteen edges. The four corner vertices have degree two and the rest of the vertices have degree three. Its degree sequence is \(3,3,3,3,3,3,2,2,2,2\text{.}\)
