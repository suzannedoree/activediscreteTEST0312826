Theorem 5.1.20. Proof Format:Proof by Contradiction.

We can prove \(P \) using proof by contradiction by adapting the following proof format.
🔗

Proof.
Suppose not. Then (state \(\neg P\)).
🔗
First, (explain why \(Q\) is true.) Therefore, \(Q\text{.}\)
🔗
On the other hand, (explain why \(\neg Q\) is true.) Therefore, \(\neg Q\text{.}\)
🔗
This is a contradiction. Therefore, \(P\text{.}\)
