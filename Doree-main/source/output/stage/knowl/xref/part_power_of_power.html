Item 2.

Simplify \((10^2)^3\) by writing it as a single power.
Solution.
Using Definition 2.4.12, we have
\begin{align*} (10^2)^3 \amp = (\underbrace{10^2\times 10^2\times 10^2}_{3 \text{ times } })\\ \amp = (\underbrace{(10 \times 10)\times (10 \times 10)\times (10 \times 10)}_{3 \text{ times } })\\ \amp = \underbrace{10\times 10\times 10\times 10\times 10 \times 10}_{6 \text{ times } })\\ \amp = 10^6\text{.} \end{align*}
🔗
Notice that the exponents multiplied: \(2 \times 3=6\text{.}\)
