Item (b).

List the positive divisors of the integer 50. Compare the prime factorization of 50 with the prime factorization of its positive divisors.
Solution.
The positive divisors of \(50=2\cdot 5^2\) are \(1, 2, 5, 10, 25\) and \(50\text{.}\) The prime factorizations of these divisors are \(1, 2^1, 5^1, 2 \cdot 5, 5^2\) and \(2\cdot 5^2\text{.}\) Table 4.4.13 lists the divisors in prime factored form and then the divisors themselves. Notice that going across a row of this table, we multiply by two and going down a column of this table, we multiply by five.
