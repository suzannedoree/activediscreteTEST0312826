14. Understand.

  Use Proof 5.2.3.2, Proof 5.1.2.1, and Proof 5.3.2.1 to prove for any integers \(a\) and \(b\) if \(a\) is even and \(b\) is odd, then \(a+b\) is odd and \(ab\) is even. Hint: Write \(a = 2k_1\) and \(b = 2k_2+1\) for some integers \(k_1\) and \(k_2\text{.}\) (You cannot use the same integer \(k\) for both.)
