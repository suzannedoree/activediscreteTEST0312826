Use cases Subsection 4.2.3 to prove that \(n^2\) mod \(3 = 0\) or \(n^2\) mod \(3 = 1\) for any integer \(n\text{.}\) Hint: Use  6 Item 6.1 and consider three cases. Note that in one case we get \(n^2\) mod \(3 = 0\) and in the other two cases we get \(n^2\) mod \(3 = 1\text{.}\) Officially, we are using a modified version of proof by cases that we revisit in Section 5.1.
