17. Understand.

In this exercise we prove Theorem 4.4.3 Item 1.
🔗
Use the contrapositive Proof 5.4.4.1 to prove that for any integers \(n\text{,}\) \(p\text{,}\) and \(m\text{,}\) if \(p \nmid n\text{,}\) then \(mp \nmid n\text{.}\) Note that this statement can be rephrased as if \(p\) is not a divisor of \(n\text{,}\) then no multiple of \(p\) is a divisor of \(n\text{.}\)
