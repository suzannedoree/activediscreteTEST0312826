Give a combinatorial proof that
\begin{equation*} \displaystyle \sum_{k=0}^n\binom{n}{k}^2 = \binom{2n}{n} \end{equation*}
for \(n \ge 1\text{.}\) Hint: Suppose there are \(n\) cats and \(n\) dogs and we want to choose \(n\) animals. Condition on \(k=\) the number of cats chosen.
