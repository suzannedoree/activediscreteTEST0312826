Example 8.5.15. Verifying the explicit form of second order recursively-defined sequence.

Consider the sequence \(a_n\) defined recursively by \(a_0= 1\text{,}\) \(a_1 =6\text{,}\) and \(a_n =6a_{n-1} -9a_{n-2}\) for \(n \ge 2\text{.}\) Use the special case of strong mathematical induction Proof 8.5.4.2 to prove that \(a_n = (n+1)3^n\) for \(n \ge 0\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
Proof. Write \(f(n) = (n+1)3^n\text{.}\) Induct on \(n\text{.}\)
🔗
First, when \(n=0\) we have \(a_0=1\) and
\begin{equation*} f(0)=(1)3^0=1\cdot 1 = 1\text{.} \end{equation*}
🔗
Similarly, when \(n=1\) we have \(a_1=6\) and
\begin{equation*} f(1)=(2)3^1= 2\cdot 3 = 6\text{.} \end{equation*}
🔗
Next, assume
\begin{equation*} a_{n-1}=f(n-1)=n\cdot 3^{n-1} \end{equation*}
and
\begin{equation*} a_{n-2}=f(n-2)=(n-1)\cdot 3^{n-2} \end{equation*}
for some integer \(n > 1\text{.}\) Then
\begin{align*} a_n \amp = 6a_{n-1} -9a_{n-2}\\ \amp = 6f(n-1)-9f(n-2)\\ \amp = 6\left(n3^{n-1}\right) -9\left((n-1)3^{n-2}\right)\\ \amp = n\cdot 6\cdot 3^{n-1}- (n-1)\cdot 9 \cdot 3^{n-2}\\ \amp = n\cdot 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 3^{n-1}- (n-1)\cdot 3^2 \cdot 3^{n-2}\\ \amp = n\cdot 2 \cdot 3^n- (n-1) \cdot 3^n\\ \amp =(2n-(n-1))3^n\\ \amp = (n+1)3^n \end{align*}
🔗
\(\Box\)
🔗
Notice it was convenient to write \(6=2\cdot 3\) because \(3 \cdot 3^{n-1} = 3^n\text{.}\) Similarly, it was convenient to write \(9 = 3^2\) because \(3^2\cdot3^{n-2}=3^n\text{.}\)
