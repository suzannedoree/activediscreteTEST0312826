Item 3.

Consider the function \(T: \mathscr{M}(\mathbb{Z}) \to \mathscr{M}(\mathbb{Z})\) defined by \(T(A) = MA\) where \(M = \left[ \begin{array}{cr} 1 \amp 2 \\ 0 \amp -1 \end{array} \right]\text{.}\) Identify the domain and codomain of \(T\) and evaluate \(T\left( \left[ \begin{array}{cc} 1 \amp 2 \\ 3 \amp 5 \end{array} \right] \right)\text{.}\)
Solution.
The domain is \(\mathscr{M}(\mathbb{Z})\text{,}\) the codomain is also \(\mathscr{M}(\mathbb{Z})\text{,}\) and
\begin{equation*} T\left( \left[ \begin{array}{cc} 1 \amp 2 \\ 3 \amp 5 \end{array} \right] \right) = \left[ \begin{array}{cr} 1 \amp 2 \\ 0 \amp -1 \end{array} \right] \left[ \begin{array}{cc} 1 \amp 2 \\ 3 \amp 5 \end{array} \right] = \left[ \begin{array}{cc} 1+6 \amp 2+10 \\ 0-3 \amp 0-5 \end{array} \right] = \left[ \begin{array}{rr} 7 \amp 12 \\ -3 \amp -5 \end{array} \right] \end{equation*}
