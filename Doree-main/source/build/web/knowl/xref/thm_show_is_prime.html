Theorem 4.4.3. Show an integer is prime.

Let \(n\) be an integer.
  1. Let \(p\) be a prime. If \(p\) is not a divisor of \(n\text{,}\) then no multiple of \(p\) is a divisor of \(n\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. If \(n\) has no nontrivial positive divisors less than or equal to \(\sqrt{n}\text{,}\) then \(n\) is prime.
