Checkpoint 2.3.32. \exerU.

Give a combinatorial proof that
\begin{equation*} \displaystyle \binom{n}{m}\binom{m}{k}=\binom{n}{k}\binom{n-k}{m-k} \end{equation*}
for any integers \(n\) , \(m\text{,}\) and \(k\text{.}\)
🔗
Hint: of \(n\) people who show up to try-outs, we will select \(m\) players for our team, of whom \(k\) will be starters.
