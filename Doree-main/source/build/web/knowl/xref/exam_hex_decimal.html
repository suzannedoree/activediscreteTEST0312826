Example 9.2.14. Conversion between decimal and hexadecimal.

 
  1. Convert \((\bs{A5F})_{16}\) to decimal.
    Solution.
    To determine the highest power of 16 involved, we count in from the right: \(16^0\text{,}\) \(16^1\text{,}\) and then \(16^2\text{.}\) Recalling that \((\bs{A})_{16}=10\) and \((\bs{F})_{16}=15\text{,}\) we have
    \begin{align*} (\bs{A5F})_{16} \amp = \underline{10}\cdot 16^2 + \underline{5}\cdot 16^1 + \underline{15} \cdot 16^0\\ \amp = \underline{10} \cdot 256 + \underline{5} \cdot 16 + \underline{} \cdot 1\\ \amp = 2655\text{.} \end{align*}

  2. Convert 695 to hexadecimal.
    Solution.
    Note that \(16^2 = 256\) is the highest power of 16 less than 695. Since \(2 \times 256 = 512\text{,}\) we subtract to get \(695 - 512 = 183\text{.}\) Next we calculate \(11 \times 16 = 176\) as the largest multiple of 16 less than 183 and subtract again to get \(183-176=7\text{.}\) Recalling that \(11=(\bs{B})_{16}\text{,}\) we have
    \begin{align*} 695 \amp = 512 + 176 + 7\\ \amp = \underline{2} \cdot 256 + \underline{11} \cdot 16 + \underline{7} \cdot 1\\ \amp = \underline{2}\cdot 16^2 + \underline{11}\cdot 16^1 + \underline{7} \cdot 16^0\\ \amp =(\bs{2B7})_{16} \end{align*}
