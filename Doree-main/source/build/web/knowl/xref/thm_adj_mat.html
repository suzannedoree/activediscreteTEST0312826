Theorem 3.2.30. Adjacency matrices.

Let \(G\) be a graph with adjacency matrix \(A\text{.}\)
  1. Let \(v\) be a vertex in \(G\text{.}\) The degree of \(v\) is equal to the sum of the entries in the row (or column) of \(A\) corresponding to \(v\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. The adjacency matrix \(\overline{A}\) of the complement \(\overline{G}\) has the property that for all \(i \neq j\text{,}\)
    \begin{equation*} \overline{A}_{i,j} = \left\{ \begin{array}{cc} 1 \amp \text{ if } A_{i,j}=\bs{0} \\ \bs{0} \amp \text{ if } A_{i,j}=1 \end{array} \right\text{.} \end{equation*}
