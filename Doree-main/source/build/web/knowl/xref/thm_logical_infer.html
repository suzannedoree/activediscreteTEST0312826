Theorem 5.5.15. Logical inferences.

For any logical statements \(P\) and \(Q\) we have the following valid logical inferences.
  1. Simplification
    \begin{equation*} P \land Q \implies P \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Addition
    \begin{equation*} P \implies P \lor Q \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Conjunction
    \begin{equation*} \left. \begin{array}{cc} P \\ Q \end{array} \right\} \implies P \land Q \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
  4. Modus ponens (m.p.)
    \begin{equation*} \left. \begin{array}{cc} P \\P \to Q \end{array} \right\} \implies Q \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
  5. Modus tollens (m.t.)
    \begin{equation*} \left. \begin{array}{cc} \neg Q \\ P \to Q \end{array} \right\} \implies \neg P \end{equation*}
