Example 1.2.9. Every integer is even or odd.

Use the Division Algorithm Theorem 1.2.2 to prove that every integer is even or odd.
🔗
Solution.
Proof Let \(a\) be an integer. By the Division Algorithm Theorem 1.2.2 with \(n=2\text{,}\) there exist unique integers \(q\) and \(r\) such that \(a=qn+r\) and \(0 \le r \lt 2\text{.}\) Note that \(r=0\) or \(r=1\text{.}\) Since \(r\) is unique, we are in exactly one of these two cases. Case 1: Assume \(r=0\text{.}\) Then \(a = 2q+0 = 2q\) is even, by the definition of even. Case 2: Assume \(r=1\text{.}\) Then \(a=2q+1\) is odd, by the definition of odd. In either case \(a\) is even or \(a\) is odd.
