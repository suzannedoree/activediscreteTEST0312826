18. Explore.

Let \(a\) and \(b\) be integers, and consider the diagonal matrix \(D=\left[ \begin{array}{cc} a \amp 0 \\ 0 \amp b \end{array} \right]\text{.}\)
  1. Calculate \(D^2\text{,}\) \(D^3\text{,}\) and \(D^4\text{,}\) and conjecture a formula for \(D^n\) for \(n \ge 1\text{.}\) Your answers will involve \(a\) and \(b\text{.}\) Make sure to show work.

    🔗
