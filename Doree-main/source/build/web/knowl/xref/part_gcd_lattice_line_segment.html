In the \(xy-\)plane, graph the line segment from \((0,0)\) to \((15,20)\text{.}\) You are welcome to use DESMOS (\url{https://www.desmos.com/} to graph the line by writing the equation and setting an appropriate range such as \(-2 \le x \le 17\) and \(-2 \le y \le 22\text{.}\)
