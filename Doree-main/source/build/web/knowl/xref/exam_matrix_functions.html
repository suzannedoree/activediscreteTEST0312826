Example 8.3.3. Functions that involve matrices.

Let \(\mathscr{M}(\mathbb{Z})\) be the set of \(2 \times 2\) matrices with integer entries.
  1. Consider the function \(f: \mathbb{Z} \to \mathscr{M}(\mathbb{Z})\) defined by \(f(n) = \left[ \begin{array}{cc} 1 \amp 0 \\ 3n \amp 1 \end{array} \right]\text{.}\) Identify the domain and codomain of \(f\) and evaluate \(f(2)\text{.}\)
    Solution.
    The domain is \(\mathbb{Z}\text{,}\) the codomain is \(\mathscr{M}(\mathbb{Z})\text{,}\) and \(f(2) = \left[ \begin{array}{cc} 1 \amp 0 \\ 6 \amp 1 \end{array} \right]\text{.}\)

  2. Consider the function \(\fn{det}: \mathscr{M}(\mathbb{Z}) \to \mathbb{Z}\) defined by \(\fn{det} \left( \left[ \begin{array}{cc} a \amp b \\ c \amp d \end{array} \right] \right) = ad-bc\text{.}\) Identify the domain and codomain of \(\fn{det}\) and evaluate \(\fn{det}\left( \left[ \begin{array}{cc} 1 \amp 2 \\ 3 \amp 5 \end{array} \right] \right)\text{.}\)
    Solution.
    The domain is \(\mathscr{M}(\mathbb{Z})\text{,}\) the codomain is \(\mathbb{Z}\text{,}\) and \(\fn{det}\left( \left[ \begin{array}{cc} 1 \amp 2 \\ 3 \amp 5 \end{array} \right] \right)=(1)(5)-(2)(3) = -1\text{.}\)

  3. Consider the function \(T: \mathscr{M}(\mathbb{Z}) \to \mathscr{M}(\mathbb{Z})\) defined by \(T(A) = MA\) where \(M = \left[ \begin{array}{cr} 1 \amp 2 \\ 0 \amp -1 \end{array} \right]\text{.}\) Identify the domain and codomain of \(T\) and evaluate \(T\left( \left[ \begin{array}{cc} 1 \amp 2 \\ 3 \amp 5 \end{array} \right] \right)\text{.}\)
    Solution.
    The domain is \(\mathscr{M}(\mathbb{Z})\text{,}\) the codomain is also \(\mathscr{M}(\mathbb{Z})\text{,}\) and
    \begin{equation*} T\left( \left[ \begin{array}{cc} 1 \amp 2 \\ 3 \amp 5 \end{array} \right] \right) = \left[ \begin{array}{cr} 1 \amp 2 \\ 0 \amp -1 \end{array} \right] \left[ \begin{array}{cc} 1 \amp 2 \\ 3 \amp 5 \end{array} \right] = \left[ \begin{array}{cc} 1+6 \amp 2+10 \\ 0-3 \amp 0-5 \end{array} \right] = \left[ \begin{array}{rr} 7 \amp 12 \\ -3 \amp -5 \end{array} \right] \end{equation*}
