Example 7.3.9. Calculating the conjugate of a partition.

 
  1. Calculate the conjugate of the partition \(3+1\text{.}\)
    Solution.
    The diagram for the partition \(3+1\) is shown on the left in Figure 7.3.10.
    🔗
    The diagram has two squares in the first column, one square in the second column, and one square in the third column. Therefore, the conjugate of \(3+1\) is the partition \(2+1+1\) shown on the right in Figure 7.3.10.

  2. Calculate the conjugate of the partition \(6+4+2+1+1\text{.}\)
    Solution.
    The conjugate of \(6+4+2+1+1\) is \(5+3+2+2+1+1\text{.}\) These partitions are drawn in Figure 7.3.11.
