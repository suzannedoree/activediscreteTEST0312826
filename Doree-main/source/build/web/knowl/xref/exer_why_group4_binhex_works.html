Checkpoint 9.2.41. \exerE.

Consider the integer
\begin{equation*} n = (a_7a_6a_5a_4a_3a_2a_1a_0)_2 = (b_1b_0)_{16}\text{.} \end{equation*}
Find a formula for \(b_1\) and \(b_0\) in terms of the \(a_i\text{.}\) Hint: Write
\begin{equation*} n=a_72^7+a_62^6+a_52^5+a_42^4+a_32^3+a_22^2+a_12^1+a_02^0 \end{equation*}
and factor \(16=2^4\) out of the first four terms.
