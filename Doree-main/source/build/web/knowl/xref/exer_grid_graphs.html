19. Explore.

For \(s,t \ge 1\text{,}\) the grid graph (or lattice graph) is denoted \(L_{s\times t}\text{.}\) Its vertices are the point,s \((x,y)\text{,}\) in the \(xy\)-plane where \(x = 1, 2, \ldots, s\) and \(y = 1, 2, \ldots, t\text{.}\) There is an edge connecting two points with the same \(x\)-coordinate when their \(y\)-coordinates differ by 1. Also, there is an edge connecting two points with the same \(y\)-coordinate when their \(x\)-coordinates differ by 1. The ladder graphs are the special case where \(s=2\text{.}\)
