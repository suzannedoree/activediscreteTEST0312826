Theorem 9.5.5. Summation formulas for powers of integers.

For a nonnegative integer \(n\) we have
  1. The sum of a constant: \(\displaystyle \sum_{k=1}^n1=n\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. The sum of the first \(n\) integers: \(\displaystyle \sum_{k=1}^nk = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. The sum of the first \(n\) squares: \(\displaystyle \sum_{k=1}^nk^2 = \frac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}{6}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  4. The sum of the first \(n\) cubes: \(\displaystyle \sum_{k=1}^nk^3 = \left(\frac{n(n+1)}{2}\right)^2\text{.}\)
