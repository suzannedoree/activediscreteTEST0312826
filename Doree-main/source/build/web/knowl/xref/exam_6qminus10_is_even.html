Example 5.2.15. \(6q-10\) is even.

Use the definition of even to prove that for every integer \(q\text{,}\) the integer \(6q-10\) is even. (This proof was Section 4.1  6 a.)
🔗
Solution.
The phrase “for every” indicates a universally quantified statement, so we follow Proof 5.2.C.2.
🔗
Proof. Let \(q\) be an integer. Then \(6q-10=2(3q-5)=2k\) where \(k=3q-5\) is an integer. By the definition of even, it follows that \(6q-10\) is even. Therefore, for every integer \(q\text{,}\) the integer \(6q-10\) is even.
