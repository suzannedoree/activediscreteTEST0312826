Item 10.

The sequence \(v\) begins \(1, 6, 15, 28, 45, \ldots\text{.}\) Hint: Factor.
Solution.
Let’s make a table comparing \(n\) and the terms of the sequence starting at \(n=1\) in factored form, in Table 6.5.16. Notice that \(v_n\) is the product of \(n\) and the odds. A possible formula is \(v_n = n(2n+1)\) for \(n \ge 1\text{.}\)
