Theorem 5.3.10. Implication equivalence.

 
  1. For statements \(P\) and \(Q\text{,}\)
    \begin{equation*} P \to Q \equiv \neg P \lor Q\text{.} \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Equivalently, for statements \(R\) and \(Q\text{,}\)
    \begin{equation*} R \lor Q \equiv \neg R \to Q\text{.} \end{equation*}
