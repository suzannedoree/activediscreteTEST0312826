Item 1.

In 1.0.1 we drew a graph representing a group of six students shaking hands with each other, as drawn again in Figure 3.2.9. How many vertices does this graph have and what does that number mean in the story? How many edges does that graph have and what does that number mean in the story?
Solution.
The graph has six vertices that represent the six students. The graph has 15 edges that represent the 15 handshakes the group did.
