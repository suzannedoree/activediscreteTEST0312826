Checkpoint 9.5.33. \exerU.

Give a combinatorial proof that
\begin{equation*} \sum_{k=1}^nk\binom{n}{k}^2 = n\binom{2n-1}{n-1} \end{equation*}
for \(n \ge 1\text{.}\) Hint: Suppose there are \(n\) cats and \(n\) dogs and we want to choose \(n\) animals including a favorite cat. Condition on \(k=\) the number of cats chosen.
