14. Understand.

The Harary graphs \(H_{4,t}\) are a family of 4-regular graphs for \(t \ge 5\text{.}\) The graph \(H_{4,t}\) is constructed from the cycle \(C_t\) by adding an edge between each pair of vertices that are two edges apart in the cycle. For example, Figure 3.2.2 shows \(H_{4,6}\text{.}\)
