  2. We are missing the last couple of rows of Table 5.1.6. What are the remaining true/false options? Fill in the \(P\text{,}\) \(Q\text{,}\) and \(R\) columns for the missing rows.

  3. In Table 5.1.6 we included an intermediate column for \(P \lor Q\text{.}\) Convince yourself that we correctly started that column and complete it. Notice that we ignore the \(R\) column.
