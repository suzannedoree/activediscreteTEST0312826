5. Understand.

  As we saw in Activity 4.4.BItem 4, Mersenne conjectured that integers of the form \(2^p-1\) are prime whenever \(p\) is prime. Primes of this form are Mersenne primes.
  1. Without using computational technology, check that \(2^2-1, 2^3-1, 2^5-1\text{,}\) and \(2^7-1\) are prime. Note that \(2^7=128\text{.}\) (If you do not know this power yet, work on learning the powers of two.)
