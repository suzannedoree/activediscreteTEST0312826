Classify all graphs with four vertices and two edges.
Solution.
If the two edges share a common endpoint, then we get a graph \(A\) that has the path \(P_3\) and an isolated fourth vertex. Otherwise, we get a graph \(B\) that has two separate copies of \(P_2\text{.}\) Both are drawn in Figure 3.5.19.
