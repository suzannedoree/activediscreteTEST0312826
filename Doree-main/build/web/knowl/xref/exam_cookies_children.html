Example 4.2.1. Cookies and Children.

Use the idea of giving 23 cookies to four children to describe \(23 \div 4\) in terms of (only) integers.
🔗
Solution.
Imagine that I have 23 cookies and four children. I want to give each child as many cookies as I can, but, to be fair, each child should have the same number of cookies as the other children. What should I do? I can give each child five cookies. That accounts for \(5 \times 4 = 20\) of the cookies, but I cannot evenly distribute the remaining \(23-20=3\) cookies without breaking them, which I do not want to do. I guess those 3 cookies are for me to eat. We might say \(23 \div 4\) is 5 cookies/child with 3 cookies remaining for me. We can write the equation
\begin{equation*} 23 = 5 \times 4 + 3\text{.} \end{equation*}
