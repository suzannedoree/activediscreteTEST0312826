Theorem 6.4.21. Proof Format: Uniqueness (Indirect).

We can prove there is at most one \(n\) with \(P(n)\) indirectly by adapting the following proof format.
🔗

Proof.
Assume \(P(n_1)\) and \(P(n_2)\text{.}\)
🔗
(Explain why \(n_1 = n_2\text{.}\))
🔗
Therefore, there is at most one \(n\) with \(P(n)\text{.}\)
