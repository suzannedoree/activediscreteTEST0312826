Activity 2.1.A. Squares and Dominoes Puzzle.

You have a large supply of \(1 \times 1\) (striped) squares and \(1 \times 2\) (gray) dominoes with which to tile a \(1 \times n\) board. That is, you want to cover every square on the board with either a square or a domino so that there is no overlap. For example, there are three different ways to tile a \(1 \times 3\) board shown in Figure 2.1.1. Notice that the order of the tiles matters.
