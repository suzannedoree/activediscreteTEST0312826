Theorem 1.1.16. De Morgan’s Law.

For statements \(P\) and \(Q\text{,}\) the negation of \(P \land Q\) is \(\neg P \lor \neg Q\) and the negation of \(P \lor Q\) is \(\neg P \land \neg Q\text{.}\) That is, we have the following logical equivalences known as De Morgan’s Laws:
\begin{equation*} \neg(P \land Q) \equiv \neg P \lor \neg Q\text{ and } \neg(P \lor Q) \equiv \neg P \land \neg Q \end{equation*}
